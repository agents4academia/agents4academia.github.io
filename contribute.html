Contribute

We welcome researchers and collaborators from all institutions and disciplines to contribute to this shared effort.

GitHub Discord @agents4academia Bluesky

Work on an existing agent

Pick a project that matches your pain — or your skills — and dive in. Each repository has open issues; start there, or open one describing what you'd improve. Pull requests are welcome from anyone, no affiliation required.

Propose a new agent

The projects so far cover literature discovery, knowledge management, benchmark reproduction, citation verification, and paper reviewing. That's five slices of the research lifecycle — there are many more. If you have a well-shaped problem and want collaborators, open an issue in the closest repository or email us — describe the problem, why agents fit, and what a two-week prototype would look like.

Join a future event

The first hackathon was invitation-only, but the community isn't. Future events will be announced on the events page. If you'd like your group or university to host one with us, get in touch.
