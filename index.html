Agents4Academia

A two-week hackathon building AI agents for academia.

Dates
14 – 26 June 2026
Venue
Department of Statistics, University of Oxford
Format
Invitation-only
Organizers
Klara Kaleb, Harit Vishwakarma, Yee Whye Teh (Oxford)
Wee Sun Lee (NUS)
Luke Ong (NTU)
Participants
Researchers from Oxford, NUS, and NTU

Participants prototype open-source AI agents to support parts of the scientific process — literature, grant discovery, cross-disciplinary translation, research coordination, and the evaluation of research agents themselves.

Code and findings will be released under permissive licences at github.com/agents4academia.
